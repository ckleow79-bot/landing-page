What this website stores in your browser

  • Language choice (English or 中文), so the next page opens in the same language.
  • Programme ribbon closed, for the rest of your visit only.
  • Page transition flag, cleared as soon as the next page opens.

These stay on your device, are not cookies sent to us, and are not used to identify or track you. You can clear them in your browser settings at any time.

Third parties

The office map on the Contact page loads from Google only when you click "Show map". Google may then set its own cookies under its privacy policy. In Chinese mode, the Chinese font loads from Google Fonts, which sees your IP address like any website request. Icons and all other files are served from this website.
