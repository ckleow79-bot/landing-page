How we work

From exposure to control, in four moves.

The same discipline runs through advisory engagements and the programmes we teach. Scroll to follow a typical engagement.

1

Diagnose

Forensic review of returns, management accounts and related-party flows. We aim to find s.113, s.140 and transfer-pricing exposures before LHDN raises them.

2

Defend or restructure

Represent you through audit, DRP and SCIT, or restructure the group to reduce the risk of it happening again. Capital statements, penalty mitigation, voluntary disclosure.

3

Run the numbers monthly

As fractional CFO we deliver two reports every cycle: an operational pack for the finance team and a decision-focused summary for the board.

4

Build the team

Train your finance staff through our Academy, from e-Invoice controls to AI-assisted close, so the capability stays in-house.
