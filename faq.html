FAQ

Questions we hear every week.

Short answers on LHDN audits, e-Invoice, fees, confidentiality and training. Anything else, ask us directly.

Can you represent my company in an LHDN tax audit or investigation?

Yes. B & K Advisory PLT acts through a licensed tax agent under s.153 of the Income Tax Act 1967. We handle desk and field audits, Jabatan Siasatan cases, Dispute Resolution Panel proceedings and appeals. Send us the LHDN letter's date and reference through the quote form; do not upload the letter itself until we set up a secure channel.

I have just received an LHDN letter. What should I do first?

Note the reply deadline on the letter and contact us as early as possible. Do not ignore it, and do not send documents to LHDN before you know what is being asked and why. Choose "Deadline within 14 days" on the quote form so we see it first.

How are your fees set?

Every engagement is quoted after a short scoping conversation, because the work depends on your facts. Use the Request a quote form or WhatsApp us, and we will come back with a written scope and fee before any work starts.

Do you only work with clients in Kuala Lumpur?

No. Our office is in Segambut, Kuala Lumpur, and we meet clients in the Klang Valley in person. Clients in other states and outside Malaysia work with us online. Public seminars also run in Johor Bahru and Kuching.

Is my information kept confidential?

Yes. Enquiries and client records are handled under the Personal Data Protection Act 2010. Please do not paste confidential figures or documents into the website form; we will arrange a secure way to receive them.

Which languages do you work in?

English and Mandarin, for both advisory meetings and training.

Can we claim your training under HRD Corp?

B & K Training Services PLT is an HRD Corp registered training provider. Whether a particular programme is claimable is shown on its listing on the Programmes page; if it is not marked, ask us before you register.

Do you run in-house training for our finance team?

Yes. Tax, SST, e-Invoice, Budget and AI-in-finance topics can be delivered at your office, in English or Mandarin, built around your own transactions. Choose "In-house training" on the quote form.

Can you help with e-Invoice if we have already started?

Yes. Most of our e-Invoice work is with businesses that are already issuing e-Invoices and want to check that MyInvois matches their accounts. Try the one-minute e-Invoice check first.
