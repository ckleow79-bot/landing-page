FAQ

Questions we hear every week.

Short answers on LHDN audits, e-Invoice, fees, confidentiality and training. Anything else, ask us directly.

Can you represent my company in an LHDN tax audit or investigation?

Yes. B & K Advisory PLT acts through a licensed tax agent under s.153 of the Income Tax Act 1967. We handle desk and field audits, Jabatan Siasatan cases, Dispute Resolution Panel proceedings and appeals. Send us the LHDN letter's date and reference through the quote form; do not upload the letter itself until we set up a secure channel.

I have just received an LHDN letter. What should I do first?

Note the reply deadline on the letter and contact us as early as possible. Do not ignore it, and do not send documents to LHDN before you know what is being asked and why. Choose "Deadline within 14 days" on the quote form so we see it first.

How are your fees set?

The first 15-minute call is free. A 60-minute consultation is from RM 1,000, credited in full to the fee if you engage us. Typical starting fees: reply to an LHDN request for documents (with a quick review and comments) from RM 3,000, General Audit [Desk Audit] from RM 5,000, Comprehensive Audit [Field Audit] from RM 8,000, Form Q appeal or DRP from RM 5,000, investigation from RM 20,000 (quoted per case), Form C from RM 2,500 a year, e-Invoice health check from RM 3,000, management accounts review from RM 3,000 a month and in-house training from RM 8,000 a day. All fees exclude 8% SST, and the final fee is confirmed in an engagement letter after scoping, before any work starts. See the full fee guide.

Do you only work with clients in Kuala Lumpur?

No. Our office is in Segambut, Kuala Lumpur. We work with clients anywhere in Malaysia: in person in the Klang Valley, by video call elsewhere, and on site in any state when the work needs it, including Sabah and Sarawak. Fees cover the work only; flights and hotel for travel outside the Klang Valley are charged separately. Public seminars also run in Johor Bahru and Kuching.

Is my information kept confidential?

Yes. Enquiries and client records are handled under the Personal Data Protection Act 2010. Please do not paste confidential figures or documents into the website form; we will arrange a secure way to receive them.

Which languages do you work in?

English, Bahasa Malaysia and Mandarin, for both advisory meetings and training.

Can we claim your training under HRD Corp?

For public seminars, claimable sessions are marked on the Programmes page; they are registered by our training partner High Impact Training Services PLT. In-house training is claimed under HRD Corp Claimable Courses (HRD CC, formerly SBL-Khas): HRD Corp pays the course fee directly to the training provider from your levy balance, for groups of 2 to 50. We confirm the approved course and claim details in your quote.

Do you run in-house training for our finance team?

Yes. Tax, SST, e-Invoice, Budget and AI-in-finance topics can be delivered at your office anywhere in Malaysia, in English, Bahasa Malaysia or Mandarin, built around your own transactions. In-house fees start from RM 8,000 a day for up to 25 participants, excluding SST. The fee covers the course only; flights and hotel outside the Klang Valley are charged separately. Choose "In-house training" on the quote form.

Can you help with e-Invoice if we have already started?

Yes. Most of our e-Invoice work is with businesses that are already issuing e-Invoices and want to check that MyInvois matches their accounts. Try the one-minute e-Invoice check first.

Do your courses count for CPD hours?

Each full-day programme carries 7 CPD hours of content. Whether those hours are recognised, and how many, is subject to each professional body's own assessment and decision, so please check with your institute. A certificate of attendance is issued for every session.

We are an accounting firm. Do you pay referral fees?

No. As a matter of professional ethics we neither pay nor accept referral fees or commissions, and we do not work under another firm's name. If you recommend B & K to a client, the client engages us directly. We are glad to train your team: see in-house training.

Can Keith speak at our event?

Yes. Keith speaks at conferences, association and chamber events, post-Budget briefings and webinars, in English or Mandarin. The fee is on request, depending on format and travel. Send the date and audience through the speaking enquiry.
