2. What we collect through this website

  • Quote and contact form: name, email, phone, company, the topic, your state, how urgent the matter is, and your message.
  • Programme registration: name, email, phone, company, job title, the programme chosen and your consent.
  • Newsletter: your email address.
  • If you WhatsApp, call or email us: whatever you choose to send.

The self-checks, the guided assistant and the language switch do not send anything to us. Your answers stay in your browser. Please do not send tax documents or financial figures through the website; we will arrange a secure channel.

3. Why we use it

To reply to your enquiry and prepare a quote, to register you for a programme and send joining details and invoices, to send the newsletter you asked for, to meet our legal, tax, accounting and professional obligations, and to keep records of our communications.

4. Whether you must give it

Fields marked * are needed to answer you or register you. Without them we cannot respond or confirm your seat. Everything else is optional.

5. Who we share it with

Only those who need it to provide the service: our own staff; training partners and venues for programmes you register for (for example High Impact Training Services); HRD Corp where you ask us to support a claim; and service providers that host this website and its database (Netlify and Supabase). Some of these providers store data on servers outside Malaysia. We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising.

6. Browser storage and third parties

We use no advertising or analytics cookies. Your browser stores a few settings locally: your language choice, whether you closed the programme ribbon, and a short-lived flag for page transitions. The office map loads from Google only when you click "Show map"; Google's privacy policy then applies to that map.

7. How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not become engagements: up to 2 years. Registration and client records: as long as the law, tax rules and our professional bodies require, then securely deleted. You can unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time.

8. Your rights

You may ask to access or correct your personal data, withdraw consent, or limit how we use it for direct marketing. Write to keith@bkcorporate.com. We may charge the fee allowed under the PDPA for an access request and will reply within the time the Act sets.

9. Security

Access to enquiry and registration records is limited to the people who need them, and connections to this website are encrypted.

10. Language and changes

This notice is available in English and Bahasa Malaysia. If the two differ, the English version applies. We will post any changes on this page with a new date.
