How registration and HRD Corp claims work.

1

Register

Use the Register button on any programme. You will receive an acknowledgement and a proforma invoice within one business day.

2

HRD Corp grant

Employers registered with HRD Corp apply for the grant on e-TRiS before the programme date. We supply the course outline, trainer profile and quotation needed for the application.

3

Attend and claim CPD

Certificates of attendance state CPD hours for MIA, CTIM and MFPC purposes. Materials are distributed digitally after the session.

4

In-house option

Any public programme can be delivered in-house, in English or Mandarin, and tailored to your industry. Minimum group size applies.
