In-house training and HRD Corp

Train your team at your office, funded from your HRD Corp levy.

In-house programmes are claimed under HRD Corp Claimable Courses (HRD CC, formerly SBL-Khas). HRD Corp pays the course fee directly to the registered training provider from your levy balance by e-Disbursement, so you do not pay the course fee upfront. Separate allowable items, such as meal or transport allowances, are claimed by the employer where they apply.
